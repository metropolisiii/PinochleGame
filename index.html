Ultimate Pinochle Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Who we are

Ultimate Pinochle is a card game published by Jason Kirby. This policy explains what information the game handles, why, who else processes it, and how to delete it. If you have a question, write to jkirby1325@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game does not ask for your email address, real name, phone number or location.
  • There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, and your information is never sold.
  • Playing alone against the computer needs no sign-in. Once you are signed in, your single-player statistics are saved with your profile.
  • To play online the game signs you in to Unity Gaming Services, and stores your profile there so it follows you between devices.
  • You can delete your account and everything stored with it from inside the game: Online, then Account, then Delete account.

Signing in

When you first go online, the game signs you in as a guest with Unity Authentication, which gives you a random player ID. You can choose to make an account with a username and password so your profile follows you to other devices. Your password goes to Unity and is never stored by the game; a sign-in token is kept on your device so you stay signed in.

Your profile

Stored with your player ID in Unity Cloud Save:

  • the name you type and the picture you choose — one of the built-in faces, or a photo you upload (cut to a small square on your device before it is sent);
  • your rating, games won and lost, tournament results, and game statistics;
  • your saved house rules;
  • the players you have muted or blacklisted, with their names as you last saw them.

Your name, picture, rating and tournament medals are public to other players: they are shown at tables, in the rated lounge, in friend lists and on the tournament leaderboard.

Playing online

Online tables, the rated lounge and tournaments use Unity's multiplayer services (Lobby and Relay). Your game traffic passes through Unity's servers, which see your network (IP) address as any internet service does. What the other players receive is what the game needs: your name, picture, rating, player ID, the cards you play and your bids. The cards in your hand are never sent to other players.

In the rated lounge, the people in it can see your name, picture, rating, which game you are looking for, your recent lounge chat and the player IDs on your blacklist (so the matchmaker can keep you apart). Lounge whispers are not private: every lounge member's device receives them, they are only not shown there.

Tournament leaderboards keep your player ID, points, name and picture for the month, and past months are archived.

Chat, voice and video

  • Table chat goes through the table's host to the people at the table. It is not stored by us; it disappears when the game ends.
  • Messages to friends are delivered through Unity's Friends service while your friend is online. The conversation history is kept only on your device and theirs.
  • Voice chat uses Vivox (a Unity service) and is streamed live to the people at your table. It is not recorded. The microphone is used only while your microphone button is on.
  • Video uses Agora and streams your camera live to the people at your table. It is not recorded. The camera is used only while your camera button is on, and it is off at the start of every game.

A host may turn chat, voice or video off at their table, and you can mute any player.

Friends

If you add friends, Unity's Friends service stores your friend list, friend requests and the players you have blocked, and shows your friends whether you are online and whether you are at a table they can join.

Reports

If you report a player, the game sends us your player ID, theirs, the reason you chose, any note you wrote and the last few chat lines your screen saw from them. Reports are stored in Unity Cloud Save so they can be reviewed, and are kept only as long as that needs.

Permissions on your device

  • Microphone — only for voice chat, and only asked for the first time you turn it on.
  • Camera — only for video, and only asked for the first time you turn it on.
  • Photos / files — only when you choose to upload a picture; only the picture you pick is read.

On your device

Your settings, a single-player game in progress, cached copies of your profile and statistics, and your chat history with friends are kept on your device. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Who processes your information

  • Unity Technologies — sign-in, Cloud Save, multiplayer, Friends, leaderboards, Cloud Code and Vivox voice. Unity's privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Agora — live video. Agora's privacy policy: https://www.agora.io/en/privacy-policy/

We do not share your information with anyone else, and we do not sell it.

Deleting your account

In the game: Online, then Account, then Delete account. This deletes your sign-in, your profile and photo, rating, statistics, saved house rules, mute and blacklist lists and friend list, and clears them from your device. It cannot be undone.

A few things are not removed by it: your entries on past tournament leaderboards, reports filed by or about you (kept for moderation), and anything other players already received, such as chat on their screens. To have leaderboard entries removed too, or to ask for deletion without the game, write to jkirby1325@gmail.com with your player name.

Children

The game includes chat, voice and video with other players and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13; if you believe a child has made an account, write to jkirby1325@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the game and at the same address, with a new effective date.

Effective October 21, 2026

What this game is

Ultimate Pinochle is a card game you can play on your own against computer opponents, or online with other people — at tables, in the rated lounge and in tournaments, with chat, voice, video, emotes and friends. It is provided by Metropolis III ("we"); by playing you agree to these terms.

Playing with others

Online play needs a free account and connects through Unity's game services. Only what the game needs is exchanged: the cards, the bids, the seat you are sitting in, and the name, picture and rating you bring to the table. Cards in your hand are never sent to the other players — each device is told only what its own player is entitled to see.

Your information

What the game stores about you, who processes it and how to delete it are set out in the Privacy Policy, in the game and at https://metropolisiii.github.io/PinochleGame/. You can delete your account and its data at any time: Online, then Account, then Delete account.

How to behave

Be kind to the people you play with. Do not harass, threaten or abuse anyone, share anything sexual or hateful, cheat, or pretend to be someone else, in chat, voice, video, your name or your picture. You can mute or blacklist anyone, and report them from the menu their picture opens.

Warnings and bans

Chat and names are filtered for offensive words, and uploaded photos are checked automatically before anyone else sees them. Breaking the rules — a photo the check refuses, repeated slurs, reports from several players, or a moderator's decision — counts as an offence. Each offence within a year goes one step further: a warning with chat turned off for 24 hours, then bans from online play of 3 days, 7 days and 30 days, then a permanent ban. Playing alone is never affected.

If you think a warning or ban is a mistake, write to jkirby1325@gmail.com.
